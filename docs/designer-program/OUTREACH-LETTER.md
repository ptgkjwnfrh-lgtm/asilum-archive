# The founding designer letter

Status: DRAFT in the house voice, terms per ruling D1 + the owner's 20 Aug
2026 economics ruling. Two rules before anything sends: (1) the
[PERSONAL LINE] is not optional — a letter without a true sentence about
THEIR work is spam and reads as it; (2) one designer at a time, never a
blast. Companion: OFFER.md goes as the follow-up when they reply, not as an
attachment on the first touch.

---

## The letter (email)

**Subject:** a booth on *ASILUM

[NAME] —

I run *ASILUM — a personalized fashion terminal. A small,
invite-only catalog where a taste engine routes independent work to readers
who are already looking for it. Discovery, commerce, community, one place.

[PERSONAL LINE — one true sentence about their work, in your own words.
Which piece, what it does, why it belongs. Write it or don't send this.]

I'm assembling the founding cohort: five to ten independent designers whose
pieces become the first real garments on the terminal. The deal, plainly:

— You keep 100% of your price on every sale. Buyers pay a 1% "founders
  fee" at checkout; there is no listing fee, no membership fee, no cost
  to join.
— Placement is optional and paid, when you want it: a hotlist booth at
  $150/month plus 15% only on the sales the booth itself drives —
  attributed, tracked, and shown to you. Even then it's taste-gated: your
  booth reaches only readers whose taste already points at your work.
— You make it, you ship it (US + Canada, tracked). We run the catalog,
  checkout, and buyer support.
— Non-exclusive. Keep your store, your stockists, everything — every piece
  we list links back to its page on your own site.
— On Shopify? Setup is nearly nothing: apply with your store, prove you
  control your domain, and your pieces import the same day — every one
  passing the same honesty gate the rest of the catalog answers to.

The honest part: order volume starts at zero. The catalog today is a
labeled demo archive while the founding cohort assembles — your work would
be among the first real inventory, and the first proof target is
deliberately small: ten completed orders, measured honestly, at least one
repeat buyer. What I can promise is placement by taste instead of ad
spend, 100% of your price on every base sale, and that we will never fake
your numbers, your availability, or your name.

If you want in — or just want to look first — reply with your store link.
Fifteen minutes and you can be live.

[OWNER NAME]
*ASILUM
https://www.asilummagazine.com
