## What *ASILUM is

A personalized fashion terminal. The Asterisk system learns what a reader
wears and what they are looking for, and puts the right independent work in
front of the right people — discovery, commerce, community in one place.
The current catalog is a labeled demo archive while the founding program
assembles; **your pieces would be among the first real garments on the
terminal.** Founding designers are the point, not an afterthought.

## The deal (ruled, not negotiable-by-mood)

- **You keep 100% of your price on every base sale.** ASILUM adds a 1%
  "founders fee" to the buyer's total at checkout — the buyer pays it,
  never you. It is non-negotiable and applies to every piece sold
  (minimum 50¢). Payment processing comes out of our side, not yours.
- **No listing fees, no membership fees, no charge to join.**
- **The hotlist booth is optional — and it is the paid program.** When you
  want placement: **$150/month booth rent** (the hotlist runs in monthly
  cycles) plus **15% on the sales your booth itself drives**. The 15%
  applies only to orders attributed to hotlist discovery — a reader finds
  you through your booth and buys — tracked in a dedicated channel and
  shown to you, never assumed. On a $100 attributed sale you keep $85; the
  buyer still pays $100 + the $1 founders fee (+ shipping and tax where
  applicable). Placement stays taste-gated: your booth reaches only
  readers whose Passport already points at work like yours — rent cannot
  buy the wrong audience. And plainly, because it protects everyone in
  the program: suspicious behaviour relating to the hotlist fee —
  attribution tampering, fee circumvention, manufactured traffic — means
  suspension and a permanent ban from the hotlist. A human reviews every
  case; the ledger is the evidence.
- **You make it, you ship it.** Orders reach you with the buyer's shipping
  details; you ship domestically (US + Canada) with tracking. ASILUM runs
  the catalog, checkout, and the support channel.
- **Non-exclusive.** Keep your own store, your stockists, everything. We
  link to your live product pages — your site's existence is a requirement,
  not a threat.
- **Invite-only.** The catalog is curated. That is the value of being in it.
- **Payouts** monthly on completed, non-refunded orders — 100% of your
  price on base sales, 85% on hotlist-attributed sales, itemized on every
  statement (first cohort: manual and personal; automated as volume earns
  it).
- **Returns** per the published policy (14-day, unworn; damaged/misdescribed
  is on us to make right with you).

## What we need from you

- Live product pages on your own site (each piece we list links to yours).
- Honest availability: "available" means it exists and ships this week;
  sold means you tell us same-day.
- Real prices, real materials, your photos.
- Ship within 5 business days with tracking.

## What the terminal does for you

- Placement by taste, not by ad spend: the Asterisk system routes your work
  to readers whose taste already points at it — and even the paid booth
  obeys taste before money.
- An editorial frame — *ASILUM reads as a magazine, not a grid of
  thumbnails.
- A verified business presence, and — when you opt in — a booth on the
  hotlist (the paid program above).
- Zero cost to find out if it works: base selling costs you nothing.

## Who fits (the targeting profile, internal)

- Independent designer or small house, US or Canada, ships domestic.
- Own site with per-piece product pages (a technical requirement — the
  catalog refuses anything without a live source URL).
- Roughly $80–$600 pieces (the band where buyers still purchase from an
  unfamiliar name and the order economics stay sane).
- Makes things with a point of view. The terminal's taste engine is real;
  it needs work with signal in it.

## The honest part

This is a founding cohort. Order volume starts at zero and the first proof
target is deliberately small: ten real completed orders, at least one repeat
buyer, measured economics. What we promise is placement, honesty, 100% of
your price on every base sale, and that we will never fake your numbers,
your availability, or your name.
