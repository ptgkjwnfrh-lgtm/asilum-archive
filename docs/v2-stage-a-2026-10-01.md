## Proposed defaults (the brief asks that they be reported)

- Ad strip rotation: 7 s per placement, no rotation under reduced motion,
  paused on hover, focus and while typing. Not announced to screen readers.
- Sheet initial height 60 vh; `EXPAND` and any focused field inside lift it
  to the full height.
- Search sheet: 12 pieces, 8 Wire posts per answer; "Search all ASILUM"
  leads to `/discover?q=` for the full page.
- ASTERISK on → `/api/discover?brain=1` (the Passport-guided order);
  off → plain relevance.

## Web deep links (a deliberate reading of "fresh launch = The Feed")

A browser opens the URL it is given. The app never restores a last-visited
tab on its own (there is no such memory; the test pins it), and a PWA
launch opens `start_url` = `/`. A link to `/cover` or `/map` opened from
outside IS a deep link in the brief's sense: the Feed is the base
destination beneath it and the dock's THE FEED is one tap away. Forcing a
redirect would break every shared link to the cover and the map.

## Stage A.2 (same day, later): the three gaps closed

- **Feed parking** — `app/page.js` parks on detail-open, page-hide and
  visibility-hidden (anchor card, offset, served order, the first 120 cards)
  under the key viewer + `/` + view + {tab, lane}; on return it paints the
  parked cards first, then the fresh feed re-finds the anchor (or its
  nearest surviving neighbour) and `settleAnchor` keeps it in place while
  images and fonts settle (one scroll at the first frame was clamped to a
  document that had not grown yet — the trap). Whatever the fresh order put
  above the anchor is offered as "N new pieces above ↑", never scrolled
  into. Wire cards carry `data-id="post:<id>"` so they anchor too.
- **Neighbourhood names** — `StreetMap.jsx` draws `map.names` (OSM place
  nodes, nearest first) after the roads: the nearest large in the display
  face, the rest small, in screen pixels; a small name under the big one's
  letters is skipped. Verified live around Bowie: Pin Oak Village,
  Essington, Bowie Town Center, Heather Hills, Sherwood Manor, Collington.
- **Front Cover placements** — `PlacementsBlock` (`Placements.jsx`) under
  the Hot List, visibly apart, reading the SAME records the dock's strip
  runs through one `usePlacements()` hook (no second advertising table).

## Known gaps after this stage

- A detail → DM → back round trip restores the feed through the parked
  record; a detail → **/checkout** → back relies on the same pagehide park.
  Sold/ended flags on parked cards are not re-checked until the fresh feed
  replaces them (within the same second on a normal connection).
- The Studio's promotion request could never list a draft: the filter looked
  for `kind === "place"` while the draft's `kind` is its venue kind. Fixed
  here (`d.kind !== "promotion"`).
- `docs/CODE-MAP.md` must be regenerated (`npm run docs:codemap`) before
  merge.
