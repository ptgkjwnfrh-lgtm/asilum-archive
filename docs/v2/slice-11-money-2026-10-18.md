## Decisions recorded (the owner may overrule)

1. **A Wardrobe sale is charged on the platform and released as a transfer**;
   a native sale stays a direct charge on the seller's connected account. The
   ledger says which (`rail`).
2. **The owner's cancellation before handoff carries no penalty** and refunds
   in full (13.14); after the carrier's delivery a sale is a sale.
3. **Offers are off by default on every piece** (13.3); turning them off
   cancels every active unpaid negotiation and tells the buyer.
